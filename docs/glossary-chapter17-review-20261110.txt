Why the four were promoted

- The coursebook's TLS example uses a temporary symmetric session key for protected client-server traffic (p.418); the syllabus includes SSL/TLS protocols.
- The syllabus explicitly requires the purpose, benefits and drawbacks of quantum cryptography. The coursebook lists specialist hardware/dedicated links, limited range and possible polarisation changes as drawbacks (p.415). The dated numerical range is deliberately not repeated.
